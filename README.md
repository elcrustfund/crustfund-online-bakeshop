# El Crustfund Landing Page

Create a web landing page for an artisan sourdough microbakery named El Crustfund. The overall aesthetic must be warm, cozy, rustic-yet-modern, using warm neutral tones (creams, warm beige, soft dough/crust hues, and touches of warm terracotta or dark cocoa). It should feel welcoming, family-driven, and high-quality.

Please build the entire landing page incorporating the following structure and features:

1. Hero Section

Main Heading: El Crustfund

Subheading: Artisan Sourdough Microbakery

Tagline: A Mom & Daughter Family-Owned Microbakery

Primary CTA Button: "View Weekly Menu" or "Place Your Order" (smoothly scrolls down to the weekly menu section).

2. About Us / Our Story

A warm, heart-felt section highlighting that we are a family-owned, mom-and-daughter team baking with passion.

Emphasize slow natural fermentation, artisan craftsmanship, and small-batch baking made with love.

3. Weekly Menu Drop & Ordering Process

Design a prominent, clear info card or banner explaining the ordering schedule before the menu items:

Ordering Window: Open Monday through Thursday until 6:00 PM sharp.

Pickup & Delivery Days: Saturdays & Sundays.

Interactive Weekly Menu Grid:

Display clean, elegant product cards for this week's drop (e.g., Artisan Sourdough Loaves, Focaccia, Sourdough Discard Cookies, and Weekly Specials).

Each product card should feature: image placeholder, item name, short description, price, and an "Add to Order" / quantity selector.

4. Order Form / Checkout Flow

A simple interactive checkout or order request form where customers can:

Choose between Pickup or Delivery.

Select their preferred fulfillment day (Saturday or Sunday).

Fill in contact details (Name, Phone Number, Email) to submit their order for the week.

5. Footer

Social media links (Instagram).

Quick recap of the ordering & delivery schedule.

A warm closing note: "Baked with love by mom & daughter."
